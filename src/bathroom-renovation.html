Bathroom renovation FAQ

Good projects start with good questions.

Can you work with a compact bathroom?+

Yes. Compact bathrooms can benefit from a clearer shower enclosure, better-scaled storage, a lighter finish palette and carefully considered fixture placement. Share photos and the room dimensions when you enquire.

Can the layout stay the same?+

It can, depending on your goals and the existing room. Keeping major elements in place may be practical, while changing a tub, shower or vanity can sometimes unlock a more useful layout. The first step is to review the space.

Can you help with finish decisions?+

Finish direction is part of defining the renovation. Bring any inspiration images or must-have materials to the first conversation so tile, cabinetry, fixtures and hardware can be considered as one composition.

What should I include in my estimate request?+

Include the project location, a few current photos, the main changes you want, and any timing or finish priorities already on your mind. Renotherapy can then identify the most useful next step.
