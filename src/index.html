Welcome to Renotherapy

A home that supports how you want to live.

At Renotherapy, we believe a well-crafted, serene and harmonious home can enhance your wellness. Our mission is to transform your living space into a sanctuary that nurtures your well-being and elevates everyday life.

Our team brings together experience across home renovation, craftsmanship and design. We work collaboratively to create functional, carefully finished spaces, with close attention to quality and detail.

We balance thoughtful workmanship with practical options for your budget, and we put clear communication and customer satisfaction at the centre of the project.

Frequently asked

Start with the right questions.

Every renovation is different. These basics can help you take the first step.

What renovation services do you offer?+

Renotherapy offers remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, exterior renovations, additions, painting, flooring, tiling, decks, custom cabinetry, door and window replacement, and siding installation.

How do I request an estimate?+

Use the short project form below, call (778) 316‑4161, or email info@renotherapy.ca. Include the type of space and a brief description of what you want to change.

What should I include in my first message?+

Share the service you need, the general location of the project, your main priorities, and any timing or material considerations already on your mind.

Can you help with material and finish decisions?+

Material choices are part of defining a practical renovation scope. Ask about the options appropriate to your project during the first conversation.
